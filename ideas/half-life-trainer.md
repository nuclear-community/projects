# 💡 Idea: Half-life trainer

**A drill app for decay-law problems** — generates random, solvable exercises (activity after *t*, half-life from two measurements, age of a sample, secular equilibrium) with instant feedback and worked solutions.

- **For whom:** students drilling [module 01](https://github.com/nuclear-community/nuclear-101/tree/main/modules/01-nuclear-physics-foundations) self-checks; also great for exam prep
- **Core features (v0.1):** three exercise types, difficulty levels, step-by-step reveal of solutions, streak tracking (local only)
- **Tech:** a static web app — no backend needed
- **Skill range:** beginner-friendly end to end
- **Stretch:** spaced-repetition scheduling; share-your-score cards for study groups
