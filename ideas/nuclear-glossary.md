# 💡 Idea: Nuclear glossary

**A community glossary of the terms that trip up newcomers** — cross section, reactivity, criticality, burnup, kerma vs. dose, fissile vs. fertile, safeguards vs. security… each with a one-paragraph plain-English definition, the formula (if any), and "commonly confused with" links.

- **For whom:** every newcomer, permanently; the glossary the whole org links to
- **Core features (v0.1):** ~50 core terms, alphabetical + browsable by module, cross-links between entries
- **Format:** structured markdown or YAML + a static site — entries are the real work; start data-first
- **Skill range:** mostly domain writing (great for people who don't want to code); light templating for the site
- **Stretch:** term-of-the-week for our Discussions; translations (TR, DE, FR…) as separate localized files
