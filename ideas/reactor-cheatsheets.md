# 💡 Idea: Reactor physics cheat sheets

**One-page formula sheets per [nuclear-101](https://github.com/nuclear-community/nuclear-101) module** — the handful of formulas, constants, and sanity-check numbers (β, 1 barn, typical moderator properties, decay heat percentages) worth having at a glance, typeset cleanly.

- **For whom:** exam-takers and anyone who wants the "useful numbers" of the field on one page
- **Core features (v0.1):** 8 sheets (one per module), print-friendly A4, consistent notation with the modules
- **Tech:** LaTeX or Typst → PDF; markdown sources in-repo
- **Skill range:** beginner-friendly; good typography matters more than code
- **Stretch:** a printable "reference card" folding the best of all eight into a double-sided sheet
